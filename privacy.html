Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

In shortCalculator answers are saved anonymously. If you join the waitlist, we keep your name and email with your answers and tell you when it opens. We never sell your information, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

This policy explains what information freedomthroughretainers.com collects, why, and what we do with it. The site is run by Consistent Creative™ Pty Ltd (ABN 37 694 627 695), "we" in this policy. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

Calculator answers. When you finish the retainer calculator, we save your answers (your monthly survival number, average job value, jobs per month, lowest and best month, and where you left the sliders) along with the date, the type of device and how you found the page, such as a link or an ad. If you don't join the waitlist, these answers are anonymous and we don't use them to identify you.

Waitlist details. If you join the waitlist, we collect your first name and email address, and keep them with the calculator answers you entered.

We don't collect payment details on this site, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we collect it

We use anonymous calculator answers, combined across everyone who uses it, to understand the businesses the program is for and to improve the calculator and the program. We use your waitlist details to email you when the next intake of Freedom Through Retainers opens and to send occasional related updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email or by contacting us.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We use a small number of service providers to run the site: Vercel hosts the site and stores anonymous calculator answers, and Kit stores our email list, including the waitlist. These providers may store data outside Australia, including in the United States, and only process it to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep anonymous calculator answers for as long as they are useful for improving the program. We keep waitlist details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or make a privacy complaint, by emailing contact@consistentcreative.com. We'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
